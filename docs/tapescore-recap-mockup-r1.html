TapeScore · Recap · Round 1

The week debriefs. The month rules.

Review grows a third scope — Today · Week · Month. The two recaps share the locked language but answer different questions: a week is 10–20 trades, too thin to prove a setup edge, so the weekly hero leads with what you did — rails, capture, the commitment you tracked — and only claims a finding when the engine clears its bar. A month is 60+ trades, so the monthly hero is finding-first, same as the Dashboard read, but scoped to a closed month you can page through. Both aggregate from the per-day stats rollup — no re-fetching the trade book.

Design noteswhat changed, how it feeds, what needs your call
Killed from the old Weekly
  • The A–F letter grade — it re-graded what TapeScore already grades, in a second vocabulary. The score is the grade.
  • The 5-card KPI row → the inline numbers ledger.
  • The day-card grid → the sessions ledger (rows scan, cards don’t).
  • Lucide icon set (Brain, Sparkles, Target…), purple/amber accents, tiny-uppercase labels.
  • “Coach Synthesis” → “The coach’s read”, with visible provenance and generated-at in PT.
How it feeds
  • Both pages aggregate trading_days.stats_json rollups — 5 rows for a week, ~22 for a month. No trade-book re-fetch for the hero, numbers, ledgers, or comparison strips.
  • Ring = the existing period aggregation over rollups; axes are the amendment-7 equal thirds.
  • Findings run computeCarryover over the window’s trades — fetched only when the engine runs.
  • Commitment pips read what Review · Today already resolves; the week just reports the loop.
  • Rails are per-trader throughout — “days kept” counts, never a rule total.
Your call
  • Weekly finding bar: the engine’s n≥10 rarely clears in one week. Lower it for weeks (n≥5, gap ≥0.5R) or keep weeks process-only?
  • “Process held” state: a fourth hero state for the factual weekly headline — in, or fold into “No clear read”?
  • Month default: last closed month (Dashboard already owns the running month) — agree?
  • Coach’s read: stays manual Generate, or auto-runs when the period closes?
  • Storage: monthly notes/synthesis need a home — extend weekly_recaps into period_recaps?
