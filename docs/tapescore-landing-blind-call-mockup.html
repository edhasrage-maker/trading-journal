Hindsight grades every winner as a good trade.

Most journals open a trade with the exit, the P&L and your notes on screen. By then you already know the answer. TapeScore puts you back at the fill with only what you could see then, so you're reviewing the decision and not the outcome.

How a blind review works

1

Import your day

Drop in your Sierra Chart trade log or a CSV from your platform. Your fills, stops and targets come in to the second.

2

Call it blind

The chart, levels and your bracket show exactly as they were at the fill second, plus the footprint and order-flow bubbles if you trade order flow. The exit, P&L, notes and tags stay hidden. Call it good trade or mistake.

3

Reveal and compare

See the result, play the tape forward from your fill, and get the coach's read. The coach is only shown the same data you were. It never sees the result.
