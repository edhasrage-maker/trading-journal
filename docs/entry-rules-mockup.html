Candles are regular 1-minute candles unless the rule says Heiken Ashi. “Closed” means the candle finished before your fill; the minute you got in is never counted as a candle. A flat candle (close = open) is not against you.

ATR = the simple average true range of the 10 one-minute candles before the one being judged. Tape Reader = ask share of everything traded in the 480 seconds before your fill, minus 50, as your research measured it. Your chart's study counts clock minutes instead; the two differ on about 1 trade in 11.

Unknown = the tape can't show it (no tick data for that time or instrument). Unknown never counts for or against you.
