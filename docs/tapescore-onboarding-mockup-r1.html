TapeScore · New-user onboarding · r1 · 2026-10-29

Trades in, one session graded, and the trader is reading their first Recap in under two minutes.

Today a new trader lands on an empty dashboard with "Import trades (CSV)". There's no help finding that file on their platform, and the landing page's "paste your chart" isn't offered. After the import they're dropped on the dashboard, often showing "0 sessions" because the 30-day window misses older history. A setup banner then asks for a 4-step wizard full of R, ATR and POC/VAH, and "Skip for now" brings the banner straight back. r1 cuts this to four screens. Each one has a single next step, and the payoff (a graded session with "For tomorrow") arrives before we ask for anything.

What changes

  • Paste is a front door, not hidden on the Log page. It is the landing page's headline promise. It reuses the Session page's paste strip; after reading the chart it asks for the P&L (extraction doesn't return it today).
  • Platform picker → that platform's export steps, next to the drop zone. No more "Detected headers: …" as the first help a trader sees.
  • "Type a trade in" = 4 boxes (direction, instrument, contracts, P&L), P&L required. Today nothing is required and a blank trade saves.
  • The file picker's Sierra account picker stays as is.

Needs work first

  • TopstepX and Rithmic aren't recognised by the CSV reader. They're marked "soon" until we have a real export to build against. Can you get one of each?
  • Tradovate / NinjaTrader parsing is still marked "validate against real exports" in the code.

  • Plain progress lines with real counts, instead of a spinner.
  • Lands on the graded Recap, where the value is, rather than the dashboard.

What changes

  • One sentence of orientation on the first Recap only, then gone.
  • Two questions instead of a 4-step wizard. They save to the same rails the wizard writes (daily_loss_limit, max_trades), and the day re-scores.
  • "Not now" means not now: it stays in the checklist ④ and never comes back as a banner. Today "Skip for now" brings the banner straight back.

  • No R, ATR, rails or POC on this screen.
