①bBringing your tags over

When the file has tag or note columns (any journal export, or a trade CSV with a "Tags" column), one extra screen appears before grading. Columns whose names match ours are already sorted. Anything else becomes its own category, named after their column, so nothing is lost or forced into our words. Most people press one button.

How it decides

  • Name match first. Setups / Playbook → Setups, Mistakes → Mistakes, Emotion(s) → Emotions, Notes → notes. Same idea as the Tradezella script you already use.
  • Anything else → a new group with their column's name. Custom tag groups already exist in Settings → Tags, so this needs no new data shape.
  • Values are split on commas and matched to existing tags ignoring case and "&/and", so "Break & Retest" and "break and retest" become one tag.
  • Account, broker IDs and other non-tag columns are offered as "Leave out".
  • The built-in groups are matched by name, not left to the trader, because the coach's read and Edge Analysis look for setups and mistakes there.

Not built yet

  • Today the importer throws tags and notes away. It keeps only the trade fields. Tradezella tags come in only through your local script, into the history table.
  • Needs a real export from TraderSync, Tradervue and Edgewonk to confirm their tag columns. Tradezella's are known.

No file, but your own tags?

  • Settings → Tags gets "Paste a list": one tag per line, into a group of your choosing. It also shows as an optional checklist item.
  • Tags can still be made on the fly while logging, as today.

  • The first-visit sentence is built from facts, not AI: the P&L, the limit they just picked, stops kept. When the score and P&L agree, it says so plainly ("You made $210 and kept to your plan").
