Can't be checked yet: your fills don't show when the stop moved. Every trade shows "unknown" here, which never counts for or against you.

TP1 = the TP1 price logged on the trade. Fills within 30 seconds of each other count as one exit. The first exit at TP1 or better is the scale-out; whatever is still on after it is the runner.

ATR = the 1-minute ATR(10) (Wilder) at entry, the one the TP2 study used. Trades that never took anything at TP1 are not judged here; how those ended is Profit kept and Stop respected.
