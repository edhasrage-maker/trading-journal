What changes, top to bottom

  1. "EOD Recap" becomes "Recap · Mon, Oct 19". "EOD" is our word, not theirs. The date picker, Share and Import stay.
  2. The score explains itself. Risk, Entry and Exit are on the card with a plain line each, and the lowest one is marked. Today you only see them by clicking the ring, which nobody finds. The cap gets a one-line reason. The word under the score matches the dashboard (Excellent / Strong / Fair / Needs work / Broke a rule).
  3. "For tomorrow" moves up to second place. It's the coach's Tomorrow lines, already sizes, counts and levels. Today they're the last thing on the page. If an ungraded day shows the Grade button here, a new user's first step is near the top.
  4. "What happened" replaces Session behavior and the heat line. Each point is one sentence with the trade number. "Ran ≥1×ATR… evaluable… handed back $1,327 from peak" becomes "Trade 3 was up +$1,020 and closed at −$307."
  5. The chart is unchanged. The Live/Screenshot toggle goes to Detailed.
  6. Trades get numbers, a word with the score, and day-level flags. A losing trade at 100 says "Good loss". Trades 2 and 3 score 100 or 44 and still carry "Sized up after a loss", because the trade Score doesn't see sizing. Without that tag, "100 / Sized up chasing it back" reads as a contradiction.
  7. The coach's read becomes two columns, Went well and Cost you, two lines each. Rule codes like P1–P5 become your rule names. The summary paragraph repeats the headline and drops out, and the • pattern lines go to Detailed. This is display only; no prompt change. The wording in this mockup is a bit plainer than the coach's. The build shows the coach's own lines with only the rule codes swapped. Plainer wording than that would need a prompt change, and those are paused.
  8. "EOD Recap" notes become "Your notes": just the box. Computed P&L and P&L override go to Detailed.
  9. Today's badges stay, smaller.
  10. The same "full breakdown" seam as the dashboard.

Moves to Detailed Tape (nothing is deleted)

  • Recording recap (BETA) and its paragraph of instructions
  • Computed P&L, P&L override
  • The lifetime heat split ("inside half you win 78%…")
  • Session analysis summary paragraph + pattern lines
  • Live / Screenshot toggle, calibration
  • Trade management (already Detailed-only)

Found on the way (bugs, in both modes)

  • The heat line contradicts its numbers. It always says "Your winners barely test the stop", even when the past-half win rate is higher. On the demo it's 78% inside vs 100% past half. It also says "all 2 trades".
  • The Week recap disagrees with itself. "No clear read: nothing here needs a lesson" sits above a coach's read that calls post-loss sizing "the week's sharpest leak".
  • "Gave back a winner: trade 3" can't be matched to anything, because the trade list has no numbers. Fixed by ⑥.
  • The dashboard's Highlights button tooltip still says "your score, one focus, and recent sessions" (out of date since r2).
